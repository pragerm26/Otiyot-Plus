# Acknowledgments

The Otiyot projects have benefited from the contributions, guidance, and support of individuals and organizations whose involvement deserves recognition. We are grateful to everyone who has helped make these projects possible.

## A Note on Acknowledgments and Attribution

**Acknowledgments are currently incomplete.** There are individuals and organizations whose contributions to the Otiyot projects deserve public recognition, but I have not yet obtained permission from everyone involved to publicly associate them with these projects.

Out of respect for their privacy and personal preferences, their names and specific contributions are not listed here at this time. This omission should not be understood as a lack of appreciation for their contributions.

As appropriate permissions are obtained, this section will be updated to recognize contributors accurately and respectfully.

## Otiyot+

Otiyot+ is a Hebrew-reading accessibility project intended to make Hebrew texts more accessible to readers with diverse learning needs.

Acknowledgments for Otiyot+ are pending confirmation and permission from the relevant contributors.

## Otiyot Press

Otiyot Press is a publishing and educational-resources project based on and extending the work of Otiyot+. It builds on the broader Otiyot project's commitment to accessible Hebrew learning.

Acknowledgments for Otiyot Press are likewise pending confirmation and permission from the relevant contributors.

## Thank You

To everyone who has contributed time, knowledge, advice, encouragement, or other assistance to either project: thank you. Your contributions are appreciated, whether or not they are publicly acknowledged here.
