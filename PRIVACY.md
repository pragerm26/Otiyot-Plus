# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 2026

Otiyot+ is designed with student privacy as a core priority. This policy explains what data the extension handles and how.

## Data Collection

Otiyot+ does **not** collect, transmit, or sell personal information.

- All Hebrew text processing and accessibility adjustments happen **locally on the user’s device**.
- Settings (color toggles, font preference, letter spacing, reading-focus mode, per-site disable, etc.) are stored only in the browser’s local storage (`chrome.storage.sync` / `chrome.storage.local`).
- No student names, school records, reading activity logs, or identifiable educational data are collected.
- The extension does not use analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party advertising services.

## Future Features

Any future optional AI-powered features will:
- Require explicit user consent before any data is shared.
- Be designed to minimize data transmission, especially anything that could identify a student.
- Prefer on-device processing whenever possible.

## Chrome Web Store & Permissions

The extension only requests the permissions necessary for its core functionality (access to page content to apply visual accessibility tools, and storage for user settings). No broader network or identity permissions are requested.

## Contact

Questions or concerns about privacy? Open an issue in the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/pragerm26/Otiyot-Plus) or contact the maintainer.

This policy may be updated as the project evolves. The most current version will always be available in this file.
