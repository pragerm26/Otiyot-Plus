# Code of Conduct

## Our Principles

Otiyot+ is a project dedicated to making Hebrew texts, including Torah texts, more accessible to those who learn them. Our community seeks to conduct itself in accordance with the principles of Torah: *derech eretz*, *kavod habriyot*, *emet*, and *machloket l’shem shamayim*.

We believe that the pursuit of knowledge carries a responsibility to act with integrity, humility, and consideration for others. We welcome contributions from people who share our commitment to the project's educational mission and who are willing to work together in a spirit of mutual respect.

## Our Standards

Members of the Otiyot+ community are expected to uphold the following principles:

- **Derech Eretz (דרך ארץ):** Treat others with courtesy and respect. Disagreements over ideas, implementations, or decisions should never become personal attacks.
- **Kavod Habriyot (כבוד הבריות):** Recognize the dignity of every person. Do not engage in harassment, humiliation, intimidation, or the deliberate degradation of others.
- **Emet (אמת):** Be honest in your communications, represent your work accurately, acknowledge mistakes, and give proper credit to the work of others.
- **Machloket L'Shem Shamayim (מחלוקת לשם שמים):** Engage in disagreement for the sake of reaching a better understanding or solution. Criticize ideas on their merits, consider opposing arguments fairly, and be willing to revise your position when warranted.
- **Anavah (ענווה):** Approach collaboration with humility. Accept constructive criticism, recognize the limits of your own knowledge, and treat beginners and experienced contributors alike with patience.
- **Achrayut (אחריות):** Take responsibility for your contributions and their consequences. Consider the needs of users, write reliable and maintainable code, and report problems honestly.
- **Kavod HaTorah (כבוד התורה):** Recognize the importance of the texts and learning that Otiyot+ seeks to make accessible. Take care to preserve textual accuracy, respect the integrity of source material, and distinguish faithfully reproduced text from modifications introduced by the software.

## Unacceptable Conduct

The following conduct is inconsistent with the principles of this project:

- Harassment, threats, bullying, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Insults, ridicule, or deliberately humiliating remarks directed at other contributors or users.
- Trolling, persistent disruption, or deliberately derailing productive discussions.
- Dishonesty, including misrepresenting contributions, taking credit for another person's work, or knowingly spreading false information about another contributor.
- Publishing private or identifying information about another person without their permission.
- Deliberately sabotaging the project, its code, or its collaborative processes.
- Disregarding reasonable requests from project maintainers concerning the orderly and respectful operation of the project.

Criticism, disagreement, and good-faith debate are not violations of this Code merely because they are strongly expressed or because others disagree with them. What matters is that such discussion remains honest, substantive, and consistent with the standards above.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for upholding this Code of Conduct and maintaining an environment in which contributors can work together productively.

Violations may be reported by opening an issue or contacting the project maintainer privately. Reports should include relevant details and, where possible, supporting evidence.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as circumstances permit. Maintainers will exercise their judgment in light of the seriousness of the conduct, its context, and any relevant history.

Depending on the circumstances, responses may include a warning, removal of offending content, temporary restrictions on participation, or a permanent ban from the project.

Project maintainers reserve the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code or undermine the project's purpose. Enforcement decisions should be made in good faith and applied consistently.

## Our Commitment

Otiyot+ exists to help people engage with Hebrew texts and pursue their learning with greater accessibility. We hope that the way we build this project reflects the values that make learning worthwhile: respect for others, honesty in the pursuit of truth, humility in the face of knowledge, and responsibility for the work entrusted to us.

We ask everyone who participates in this project to help uphold these principles.
