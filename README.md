# Otiyot+

**Making Hebrew reading more accessible, one letter at a time.**

Otiyot+ is a Hebrew reading-accessibility browser extension designed to help English-speaking students, particularly Jewish students, learn to read Hebrew with greater confidence and independence.

Hebrew reading presents unique challenges for beginners, from unfamiliar letters and vowel markings to the visual complexity of traditional Jewish texts. For students with dyslexia and other reading difficulties, these challenges can make learning to read Hebrew especially demanding.

Otiyot+ helps address these barriers through customizable visual accessibility tools, including color-coded *niqqud*, a dyslexia-friendly font, adjustable letter spacing, and reading-focus mode.

**Our goal is simple: make Hebrew more readable and help every student engage more confidently with Jewish texts.**

🔗 **Repository:** [github.com/pragerm26/Otiyot-Plus](https://github.com/pragerm26/Otiyot-Plus)

## ✨ Features

Otiyot+ provides tools to make Hebrew text easier to read and navigate.

- **🎨 Niqqud Color-Coding** — Uses color to distinguish Hebrew vowel markings, helping readers identify vowels and recognize patterns more easily.
- **📖 Dyslexia-Friendly Font** — Offers a reading-friendly typeface designed to make letters easier to distinguish.
- **↔️ Adjustable Letter Spacing** — Increases the space between letters to reduce visual crowding and improve readability.
- **🎯 Reading-Focus Mode** — Helps readers concentrate on the text by reducing visual distractions.
- **↩️ Right-to-Left Support** — Accommodates Hebrew's natural reading direction.

The goal is not to change the Hebrew text itself, but to make its visual presentation more accessible to individual readers.

## 🎯 Who Is Otiyot+ For?

Otiyot+ is designed for anyone who wants a more accessible Hebrew reading experience, including:

- **Jewish day-school and yeshiva students** learning to read Hebrew.
- **Students learning Hebrew as a second language.**
- **Hebrew-school students** developing their reading skills.
- **Students with dyslexia or other reading difficulties.**
- **Teachers and educators** looking for ways to support diverse reading needs.
- **Anyone** who finds traditional Hebrew text difficult to read.

Whether you're learning to read a siddur, studying Tanach, or working through other Hebrew texts, Otiyot+ aims to make the experience more approachable.

## 🏫 Developed With Educator Feedback

Otiyot+ is being developed with input from educators and specialists who work with students with reading disabilities.

After discussions with specialists at [Shalshelet](https://shalsheletnj.org/), a school supporting Jewish students with learning disabilities, several opportunities for future development emerged.

Their feedback helped inform the project's direction, with a focus on practical tools that can support Hebrew reading both in the classroom and during independent study.

### Planned Improvements

The following features represent areas of planned development:

- **🎼 Trop and Cantillation Support** — Highlighting *ta'amei hamikra* (cantillation marks) to help students follow the structure and rhythm of Torah reading.
- **🔎 Shoresh Highlighting and Search** — Identifying and highlighting Hebrew root words (*shorashim*) to help readers recognize recurring word patterns and connections between words.
- **🖥️ Desktop Application** — Providing a dedicated environment for preparing and customizing Hebrew texts, including Tanach, Mishnah, Gemara, and siddurim.
- **📄 Accessible PDF Export** — Generating print-ready Hebrew reading materials with accessibility adjustments preserved.

These improvements aim to extend Otiyot+ beyond browser-based reading and make it a more comprehensive tool for accessible Hebrew education.

## 🔒 Privacy

Otiyot+ is designed with student privacy in mind.

Our privacy principles include:

- **No sale of personal information.** Otiyot+ does not sell users' personal information.
- **Local-first processing.** Hebrew text and reading activity are intended to remain on the user's device by default.
- **No collection of identifiable educational records.** Otiyot+ is not designed to collect student names, school records, or identifiable educational data.
- **User control over future AI features.** Any future optional AI-powered functionality should require explicit user action and be designed to minimize data sharing, particularly information that could identify a student.

These principles describe the project's intended privacy approach. Please consult the extension's current implementation and privacy documentation for details about the data it actually processes.
