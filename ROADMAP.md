# Roadmap

This document outlines planned and potential future work for Otiyot+. Priorities may shift based on user feedback, educator input, and available development time.

## Near-Term / High Priority

- **Trop / Cantillation Support**  
  Highlight *ta’amei hamikra* so students can better follow the structure and melody of Torah reading.

- **Shoresh Highlighting & Search**  
  Identify and highlight Hebrew roots (*shorashim*) to help readers see patterns across related words.

- **Improved PDF Workflow**  
  More robust handling of complex layouts, better font embedding, and easier teacher-facing tools.

- **Accessibility Polish**  
  Continued refinement of color contrast, keyboard navigation, and screen-reader friendliness.

## Medium-Term

- **Desktop Application**  
  A standalone environment for preparing and customizing Hebrew texts (Tanach, Mishnah, Gemara, siddurim) with the same accessibility features.

- **Accessible PDF Export Improvements**  
  Higher-quality print-ready materials that preserve all accessibility settings.

- **Google Docs / Classroom Integration**  
  Smoother “Apply to Google Doc” experience and possible Classroom sharing helpers.

## Longer-Term / Exploratory

- Optional on-device or privacy-preserving AI features (with explicit consent).
- Support for additional Hebrew learning platforms and texts.
- Community-contributed color schemes and font options.
- Localization of the extension UI itself.

## How This Roadmap Is Updated

Items are driven by:
- Feedback from students, teachers, and specialists (especially Shalshelet and similar programs)
- Real-world testing in classrooms and independent study
- Technical feasibility and privacy considerations

Suggestions are always welcome — open an issue or discussion on GitHub.

---

**Goal remains the same:** make Hebrew reading more accessible, one letter at a time.
